Rupio – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Rupio ("the app") is a personal expense, credit card, EMI and savings tracker. It is built to keep your money data on your phone only.

Summary

  • No account, no sign-up, no login to any bank.
  • Everything you enter is stored only on your device.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no data sold or shared.
  • The developer runs no server and never receives your data.

What the app stores

The accounts, cards, investments, loans, savings goals, budgets and transactions you add, and your settings (theme, language, reminders). This data is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Receipt photos you attach are copied into the app's private folder.

Statement PDFs you open or share with the app are read on the phone and are not uploaded anywhere.

Gmail (optional, APK from GitHub only): if you connect Gmail, the app asks Google for read-only access to bank alert emails and statement PDFs. They are downloaded straight from Google to your phone and processed there. You can disconnect at any time from the app or from your Google account. The Google Play version does not offer Gmail access.

Sharing

The app does not share data with anyone. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to: exporting a CSV, Excel or PDF report, or saving a backup, which you send wherever you pick (for example Google Drive or email).

Deleting your data

Delete entries, accounts, cards, loans or goals inside the app at any time. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything it stored. Backups you exported yourself are not affected.

Contacting support

Contact support and Report a bug in Settings open your own email app with a draft addressed to the developer. A bug report draft includes the app version, Android version and phone model so the problem can be found. You see the whole email before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it yourself.

Children

The app is meant for adults managing their own money and is not directed at children.
